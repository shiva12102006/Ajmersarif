Our Services

Experience the Blessings of Dargah Sharif

Every guest who arrives at the blessed Dargah of Hazrat Khwaja Moinuddin Hasan Chishti (Q.S.A.) is received with respect, warmth, and the spirit of the Sufi Chishti tradition.

01

Guided by the Traditions of the Chishti Silsilah

We provide visitors with authentic information about the blessed life, teachings, and legacy of Khwaja Gharib Nawaz (Q.S.A.), enabling guests to connect with the message of love, compassion, and service.

02

Traditional Tabarrukat and Blessed Offerings

We provide traditional Tabarrukat/Prasad, Candles, Flowers and other blessed souvenirs associated with the customs and traditions of Dargah Sharif, preserving the spiritual heritage with sincerity and reverence.

03

Comfortable and Hassle-Free Visits

Our dedicated assistance helps devotees and guests navigate the sacred premises of Dargah Sharif with ease, ensuring a peaceful and meaningful ziyarat/darshan experience.

04

Assistance with Accommodation

Whether you are visiting for a day or staying longer, we help guests find suitable accommodation according to their preferences and requirements, making their journey comfortable and worry-free.

05

Traditional Services and Convenient E-Services

For visitors unable to attend personally, we facilitate both traditional and online arrangements for:

  • Chadar and Flower Offerings
  • Langar and Nazr-o-Niyaz
  • Special Prayers and Devotional Services
  • Other customary offerings performed with sincerity and respect
06

Deg Booking, Cooking, and Distribution

We make the entire process of Deg offerings simple and convenient. From booking and preparation to the traditional cooking and distribution of the blessed food within Dargah Sharif, every arrangement is carried out with care and according to established customs.

Serving Guests with Sincerity

Inspired by the timeless teachings of Khwaja Gharib Nawaz (Q.S.A.), we strive to make every visitor’s journey one of peace, solace, devotion, and spiritual fulfilment.
